## Round

Round represents one DOE iteration. A Round owns its own split design, execution, data, and current-round analysis boundary.

## Evaluation State

Evaluation State describes the readiness or validity of DOE data. `PARTIAL`, `NO_DATA`, `N/A`, `FAILED`, and `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` are distinct semantics and must not be flattened.

## Current Round Report

Current Round Report equals one Round plus one read-only Round Snapshot. It is current-round fact analysis, not live execution and not a final decision workflow.
