# Dashboard 01 Analysis

## Source

`src/registry/new-york-v4/blocks/dashboard-01/`

This is a shadcn registry reference bundled with the repository. It is not a
DOE product screen and must not be copied as DOE business truth.

## Observed Visual Facts

- A sidebar and compact header create a stable enterprise workspace frame.
- Card groups provide scan-friendly summaries.
- Tables, filters, and charts share a restrained neutral surface treatment.
- The example contains generic product copy, navigation, and workflow that do
  not belong to DOE by default.

## Candidate Extractions

- Enterprise information hierarchy and restrained surface treatment may inform
  a future DOE Pattern after a DOE use case confirms them.
- The dashboard's business copy, navigation model, and data interactions are
  rejected as direct product rules.
