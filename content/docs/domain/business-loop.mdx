---
title: Information Architecture
description: The minimal DOE business loop and documentation boundary.
---

DOE applications should preserve the difference between planning, execution, observed data, and analysis.

## Business Loop

1. Define the Round boundary.
2. Plan split design and intended conditions.
3. Execute and observe actual conditions.
4. Evaluate data readiness and quality.
5. Analyze current-round facts from a read-only snapshot.

Planned Condition must not be presented as Actual Condition. Current Round Report is read-only and should not behave like an execution workflow or a final decision flow.
